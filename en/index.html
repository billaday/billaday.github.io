01 ABOUT

About

Understanding offshore foundations
through soil mechanics

I am an Associate Professor and PhD Supervisor at the School of Naval Architecture, Ocean and Civil Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, specializing in offshore geotechnical engineering.

My research addresses soil mechanics problems in offshore wind energy and marine infrastructure, focusing on advanced offshore soil testing and in situ sensing, soil–structure interface mechanics, and coupled wave–seabed–foundation analysis. I combine experimental and numerical methods with engineering practice, and bring research into teaching and student training.

02 RESEARCH

Research

Connecting soils, interfaces and foundations
with offshore engineering needs

Offshore ground and foundations

I.

Offshore soil testing and sensing

TESTING & SENSING

Developing advanced soil testing and in situ penetration methods for complex marine environments, integrating laboratory tests with field data.

II.

Soil–structure interface mechanics

SOIL–STRUCTURE INTERFACE

Investigating how drainage, shear rate, roughness and cyclic loading influence interface strength and its underlying mechanisms.

III.

Wave–seabed–foundation interaction

WAVE–SEABED–FOUNDATION

Using multiphysics simulations to study seabed response, soil liquefaction and offshore foundation capacity under wave loading.

Applications

Offshore wind foundations   /   Marine renewable energy   /   Coastal hydraulic structures
