01 ABOUT

About

Understanding offshore foundations
through soil mechanics

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Ocean and Civil Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research focuses on offshore geotechnical engineering.

I investigate how seabed soils behave and interact with offshore foundations. My work spans marine soil testing and characterisation, soil–structure interface mechanics, and wave–seabed–foundation interaction. I combine laboratory experiments, in situ measurements and numerical modelling to address engineering challenges, while bringing these research perspectives into teaching and student mentoring.

02 RESEARCH

Research

From soil behaviour and interface mechanics
to offshore foundation performance

Seabed–foundation systems

I.

Offshore soil characterisation

TESTING & SENSING

I develop laboratory tests, in situ sensing techniques and penetration testing methods to characterise offshore soils and relate their behaviour to seabed conditions.

II.

Soil–structure interface mechanics

SOIL–STRUCTURE INTERFACE

I investigate how drainage conditions, shearing rate, surface roughness and cyclic loading govern soil–structure interface strength, with a focus on the underlying mechanisms.

III.

Wave–seabed–foundation interaction

WAVE–SEABED–FOUNDATION

I use coupled numerical models to examine wave-induced seabed response, soil liquefaction and foundation bearing capacity, linking seabed processes with offshore foundation performance.

Applications

Offshore wind foundations   /   Marine renewable energy   /   Coastal and marine structures
