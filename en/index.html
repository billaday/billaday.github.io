01 ABOUT

About

Marine soil behaviour
and offshore foundations

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Ocean and Civil Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research focuses on offshore geotechnical engineering.

My research covers marine soil characterisation, soil–structure interface mechanics and wave–seabed–foundation interaction. I combine laboratory testing, in situ measurements and numerical modelling to investigate soil behaviour and foundation response. This work addresses geotechnical challenges in offshore wind and marine infrastructure, and informs my teaching and supervision.

Research interests

I.

Marine soil characterisation

TESTING & SENSING

I develop methods for laboratory testing, in situ sensing and penetration testing of marine soils. By combining laboratory and field measurements, I investigate their engineering properties and mechanical behaviour.

II.

Soil–structure interface mechanics

SOIL–STRUCTURE INTERFACE

I study the shear behaviour of soil–structure interfaces under different drainage and loading conditions. My work examines the effects of shearing rate, surface roughness and cyclic loading on interface strength.

III.

Wave–seabed–foundation interaction

WAVE–SEABED–FOUNDATION

I use numerical modelling to investigate wave-induced seabed response and soil liquefaction. I examine how these processes affect the bearing capacity of offshore foundations.
